## Strengths

- Variable party size is supported through bodies, positioning, civilians, and social consequences rather than artificial DC inflation.
- The candidate repeatedly avoids unsupported damage numbers, exhaustion defaults, or custom stat blocks made only to fill gaps.
- Omen and Joint Rite mechanics remain procedural and civic instead of being overconverted into forced check sequences.
- Map 4 keeps the Chapter 08 moon-road logic source-backed and bounded.

## Outstanding mechanics questions

3. `Map 4 Moon Road` uses an environmental setback instead of candidate-only damage if activation is forced under imbalance; that remains the safer pilot choice.
4. Any future Arc III Foundry package would still need the separate F-N-001 disposable-world validation gate.
