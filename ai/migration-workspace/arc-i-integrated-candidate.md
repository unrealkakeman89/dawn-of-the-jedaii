### Encounter Scaling (short)

Optional combat (panicked beast, panic brawl) and environmental hazards should scale to:

- Number of player characters at the table
- Level 9 capability
- Whether allied NPCs are present and helping
- Encounter role: combat threat, social pressure, exploration puzzle, or atmosphere only

Prefer adjusting creature count, NPC intensity, or rounds of pressure over fabricating new stat blocks. Null-corridor and Storm Clock remain the ship's primary discipline tools after lethal violence spikes (source Ch04).

## Central Reference Summaries (Arc I local)

### Boon Scaling Policy

**Pool:** Six Tho Yor boons from Ch11. Do **not** invent new boons for this pilot.

| Party size | Allocation |
|------------|------------|
| **Any** | One starting boon vision per player character (unless Kakeman89 later authorizes otherwise). |
| **Fewer than 6** | Assign one unique boon vision per player character. Unassigned boons remain aboard for later discovery, rivals, allies, or future story. |
| **Exactly 6** | Each player character may receive one unique boon from the pool. |
| **More than 6** | Do not duplicate or invent boons. Mark allocation as requiring GM selection, delayed assignments, group-linked visions, or later design. This requires Kakeman89's decision before inventing or duplicating boons. |

**Find locations (Ch11):** Dormitory niche, Confluence handrail, Threshold mural, Machine-Spirit tray, Gallery rail, Core alcove.

**Full pool names:** Milk of the Sword, Thread of the Uncut Hour, Coin of the Second Mouth, Ash-Glass Lens, Quiet Paw Charm, Bowl of First Rain.

### Storm Clock (brief)

Optional 6-segment **Imbalance Storm Clock** (Ch10). Advance on notably imbalanced acts; at 3 = omens (App B); at 6 = Force weather event, reset to 2. Acts of deliberate Balance clear 1–2 segments. Aboard ship: affects null-corridor frequency, glyph difficult terrain on Map 1 when Storm Clock ≥3 (Ch23), council tone. **Full rules:** Ch10 § Optional Imbalance Storm Clock; Ch14 references.

### Economy: craft/favor only

Republic credits and homeworld coin **buy nothing** aboard or in first camps. Gear via self-craft or NPC crafter commission (Torv, Shekk-Arra, Miri, Vesh & Loom—Ch22) paid in materials, favors, protection, or status. Enforce on first purse-open. **Full rules:** Ch10 Money is useless; Ch13 Episode 1 checklist.

### 31.5 hour ship clock

Cabin lighting and sleep-pressure follow **Tython's day/night**—**31.5 standard hours** per cycle—even before landing. Homeworld days feel wrong; the ship teaches the green world's rhythm. Meal times argue; tempers fray on extra hours. **Full context:** Ch04; Ch09 calendar.

### Senn-Vora default Speaker (brief portrayal)

**Senn-Vora — Tho Yor of the Quiet Peak** (party ship default). Talid (Dai Bendu); dust-pale robes; voice like measured prayer. Moves paced—not meek. Arrives in silence after the first punch. Wants an Order that listens; fears the Call was vanity. Hook: stop a brawl without blood. May later offer the word *Je'daii*. If the party ship epithet differs from the calling, reflavor Quiet Peak or keep it. Calling-to-Speaker assignments are not decided yet. **Full stat:** Ch22 §1.

### Kwa foreshadow murals (must-show Session 2)

Wall reliefs in Machine-Spirit / Vault ozone corridor:

- Ring-within-ring gate
- Two destination disks (bright / dark—rhyme with Ashla/Bogan **concepts**, no moon names in read-aloud)
- Nine-fold refusal marks
- Many species touching one circle together
- Script feels **wrong for Tho Yor** (softer math, older curves)

Player characters may sketch/memorize. Do **not** open Gate Arc I. Later Ch08 reward if cited: advantage on first activation check **or** auto-refuse one illegal destination. **Full spec:** Ch04 §5; Machine-Spirit Scene below.

## Session 1 — The Call Completed

**Arc beat:** First footsteps aboard (or wake aboard). Threshold + Dormitories tour. Meet pilgrims. Ship will not go back. First boon recovery.

**Cold open options (player agency):**

| Option | Setup | Agency handling |
|--------|-------|-----------------|
| **0 (optional)** | Read **opening crawl** (App C—player-safe, no destination name), then cut to play | Players may decline crawl and start in-scene |
| **A — Final boarding** | Final climb into waking pyramid; doors seal; cut to Threshold Halls | Players who want to roleplay the Call get one short beat; then cut—Call-door sealed regardless |
| **B — Wake aboard** | Fragmented memories; already on dais or in Threshold | Players supply memory fragments; GM confirms Call happened, details fuzzy |

**If players bypass both:** Start at Threshold read-aloud regardless—they are aboard.

**Strong start:** Threshold Halls read-aloud (Ch21). End on the handle-less door.

**Must-play beats:** Threshold + Dormitories (Map 1 within Level 1); a number of vivid NPCs appropriate to the party and scene (typically two or three); language/custom clash; ship won't open to past; at least one boon recovered (or first recovery beat started).

**Combat (optional):** Panicked beast in W corridor, or panic brawl → null-corridor after ~3 rounds if lethal intent spikes (source Ch04). Scale number of creatures or NPC intensity for party size and level 9.
