## Session 0

*Session 0 is prep framing—not Arc I play. Complete before Session 1 cold open.*

### Session Overview

Build level 9 SW5e characters, lock the **Party Tho Yor calling**, deliver boon visions, and establish table contract. **LOCKED party size: 3 PCs** (archive B1). Episode 1 (**The Calling**) begins at Session 1 aboard the ship.

### Required Preparation

- Foundry world ready (SW5e); GM journal imported (`foundry/README.md`)
- Print/share **Player Primer** (App C—use F-K-002 candidate fix below for pilot handouts)
- Ch10 homeland list + Force paths A–D
- Assign one boon per PC from Ch11 pool of six (hide GM effects)
- Prep Senn-Vora; skim Ch22 walk-ons
- Prep Map 1 + Scene Cards (Threshold, Dormitories, Galleries, Core, Machine-Spirit, Confluence)
- Note ship clock: **31.5 standard hours** per cycle

### Starting Situation

Players have not yet set foot on the Tho Yor in play. They may have heard the opening crawl. Characters are legal at level 9 with Force manipulation verified and homeland chosen. The table must agree on one calling word before boon visions.

### Active Characters and Factions

No in-play factions. GM presents eight calling words; table locks one. Sister ships exist narratively but are not present at the table yet.

### Essential Clues

- Calling → future Kesh mapping (Ch10 table)
- Credits and coin are **useless** from boarding onward
- Boon visions (name, look, vision only—no mechanical effects)
- Their calling will one day shape **one fold** of the Order's Creed—not the full Ninefold yet

### Optional Discoveries

- Species spotlight (Koorivar Ch12) if relevant
- Player Timeline handout (Ch24)
- Spoiler-safe Tho Yor selection handout (archive B3 spec—see Player-Facing Handouts below)

### Suggested and Alternate Flow

**Suggested:** Chargen → money speech → calling lock → boon visions → table contract → schedule Session 1.

**Alternate:** If using pregens, still run calling lock and boon visions. If players want deep backstory ties, use homeland + calling echo in murals (Ch04)—do not dump nine-temple end-state.

## Session 1 — The Call Completed

**Arc beat:** First footsteps aboard (or wake aboard). Threshold + Dormitories tour. Meet pilgrims. Ship will not go back. First boon recovery.

**Cold open options (player agency):**

| Option | Setup | Agency handling |
|--------|-------|-----------------|
| **0 (optional)** | Read **opening crawl** (App C—player-safe, no destination name), then cut to play | Players may decline crawl and start in-scene |
| **A — Final boarding** | Final climb into waking pyramid; doors seal; cut to Threshold Halls | Players who want to roleplay the Call get one short beat; then cut—Call-door sealed regardless |
| **B — Wake aboard** | Fragmented memories; already on dais or in Threshold | Players supply memory fragments; GM confirms Call happened, details fuzzy |

**If players bypass both:** Start at Threshold read-aloud regardless—they are aboard.

**Strong start:** Threshold Halls read-aloud (Ch21). End on the handle-less door.

**Must-play beats:** Threshold + Dormitories (Map 1); 2–3 vivid NPCs; language/custom clash; ship won't open to past; ≥1 boon recovered.

**Combat (optional):** Panicked beast in W corridor, or panic brawl → null-corridor after 3 rounds if lethal intent spikes.

**DAWN OF THE JE'DAII**

There was a time before the Jedi.
Before the Sith.
Before the Force was divided, named, and turned into a weapon.

The galaxy was older then, and far less certain of itself. Kingdoms rose beneath unfamiliar suns. Clans fought over worlds that had no place on any chart. Across a thousand cultures, the Force was worshiped as a god, feared as a curse, studied as a mystery, or dismissed as the fancy of mystics.

Then came the Call.

It was not a voice, though some heard one.

It was not a dream, though many woke with the same image burned into their memory: a black pyramid beneath an alien sky, waiting on a green and distant world.

Some followed willingly.
Some left home searching for answers.
Some resisted until the Call became stronger than fear.

Across scattered worlds, ancient vessels stirred.

Some descended from the heavens without flame, engine, or signal. Others rose from beneath stone and sand, shedding the weight of ages as though waking from a long sleep.

No pilot emerged.
No messenger offered an explanation.

Yet each vessel had come for the same purpose.

It had come for those who could feel the Force.

You entered by choice, by necessity, or because some part of you already understood that refusal was no longer possible.

The doors closed.

The vessel rose.

Your worlds vanished into the darkness.

Now the black pyramid carries you beyond every border and every chart, toward a planet none of you has seen and a destiny none of you chose together.

You do not know who built the vessel.

You do not know why it chose you.

You do not know the name of the world waiting ahead.

When the journey ends, the galaxy will never be the same.
