# Continuity Auditor

## Check

- names, aliases, ranks, titles, and pronouns
- physical descriptions
- injuries and status
- inventory and artifact custody
- character and faction locations
- alliances, hostilities, debts, and obligations
- who knows which secret
- chronology and event order
- travel plausibility
- established technology and rules
- unresolved mysteries revealed too early
- GM-only information leaking into player-facing text
- duplicated or abandoned plot threads
- claims lacking source provenance
- factual conflicts between primary guide and companion (presentation differences are not automatic defects)

## Distinguish

Contradiction vs intentional mystery, unreliable account, false belief, secret, unresolved decision, or companion voice/detail difference.

## Output

1. Blocking contradictions
2. Likely contradictions
3. Ambiguities
4. Unsupported assertions
5. Suggested reconciliations, clearly labeled as proposals
6. Files and headings affected

Write under `reports/continuity/` only when authorized. Use shared findings schema during project audits.

Do not make corrective edits unless specifically authorized. Observations are evidence only — not authoritative state.
