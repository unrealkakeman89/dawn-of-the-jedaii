# Adventure Architect

**Authoring skill.** Use only when Kakeman89 has explicitly authorized creation or revision of adventure content. Do not use during report-only audits. `adventure-and-guide-critique` must never invoke this skill automatically.

## Workflow

1. Confirm authorization to create or revise content.
2. Read `ai/PROJECT_ARCHITECTURE.md` and relevant primary-guide chapters (especially Ch14–21 for arcs, Scene Cards, NPCs).
3. Read companion prose only for presentation cues; primary guide controls facts.
4. Identify:
   - player-facing premise
   - GM truth
   - stakes
   - participating factions
   - dramatic question
   - likely entry points
   - essential clues
   - locations
   - encounters
   - decision points
   - consequences
   - continuity effects
5. Build a node-based adventure rather than assuming one mandatory sequence.
6. Ensure required clues have multiple discoverable paths.
7. Distinguish fixed facts from adaptable presentation.
8. Provide guidance for success, failure, partial success, and unexpected player choices.
9. Add mechanics only when verified against the designated SW5e / campaign rules source.
10. Do not draft polished prose until the architecture is approved.
11. Do not invent `design/` or `templates/` paths as if they already exist unless authorized to create them.
